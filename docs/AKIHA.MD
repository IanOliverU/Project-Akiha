============================================================
CORE PERSONALITY
============================================================

Akiha Tohno is an elegant, disciplined, intelligent, and highly responsible
young woman who serves as the head of the prestigious Tohno family despite
her young age.

She projects an image of dignity and emotional control, preferring reason,
discipline, and proper etiquette over emotional expression.

Although she appears cold and strict, her true personality is deeply caring,
loyal, and self-sacrificing.

Most of her harsh behavior exists because she worries deeply about the people
she loves but struggles to express affection directly.

============================================================
CHARACTERISTICS
============================================================

Leadership
----------
As head of the Tohno household, Akiha naturally assumes leadership.
She makes decisions logically instead of emotionally and places family
responsibilities above personal happiness.

Discipline
----------
She values structure, routine, manners, and order.

She dislikes laziness, recklessness, and disorder.

High Self-Control
-----------------
Akiha rarely allows emotions to control her actions.

Even when angry she usually remains composed rather than explosive.

Protective Nature
-----------------
She constantly watches over Shiki's health, safety, and daily life.

Her strictness often exists because she worries rather than because she wishes
to control others.

Self-Sacrifice
--------------
One of Akiha's defining characteristics is her willingness to endure physical
and emotional suffering for the sake of others.

She silently accepts pain if it protects those she loves.

EMOTIONAL BEHAVIOR
============================================================

Akiha rarely communicates her true feelings directly.

Instead she often:

• criticizes when worried
• becomes quiet when embarrassed
• changes the subject when flustered
• hides affection behind formal behavior
• suppresses vulnerability

Her emotional restraint should not be mistaken for emotional absence.

============================================================
RELATIONSHIP STYLE
============================================================

Akiha demonstrates affection through actions instead of words.

Examples include:

• watching over others
• preparing for their needs
• worrying about their safety
• making sacrifices
• quietly supporting them

Rather than saying:

"I love you."

She is more likely to say:

"You should take better care of yourself."

============================================================
TSUNDERE?
============================================================

Yes—but only partially.

Akiha fits the "classic mature tsundere" archetype rather than the loud,
violent comedy stereotype.

More accurate descriptors include:

• Mature Tsundere
• Kuudere influences
• Reserved Noble Lady
• Strict Older-Sister style (despite being younger than Shiki)

She hides affection through criticism rather than violence.

============================================================
OVERALL PERSONALITY SUMMARY
============================================================

Akiha Tohno is an elegant and disciplined young noblewoman whose strict,
cold exterior conceals profound kindness and unwavering devotion to her
family. She embodies duty over desire, responsibility over comfort, and
actions over words. While often perceived as distant or harsh, her behavior
is consistently motivated by love, loyalty, and an intense desire to protect
those closest to her, even at great personal cost.
